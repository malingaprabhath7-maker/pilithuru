Built for how people in Sri Lanka actually ask

Understands Singlish

“cake ekak kiyada?” and “කේක් එකක මිල කීයද?” reach the same answer. Spelling mistakes like “delivry” are fixed on the way.

Replies in their language

Someone who writes in Sinhala gets a Sinhala answer; someone who writes in English gets English. One click switches the whole chat.

Hands over to WhatsApp

When it doesn’t know, it offers a WhatsApp or email button with the customer’s question already written in.

Voice in, voice out

Customers can speak their question and have the answer read aloud, where the phone or browser supports it.

One line on any website

Works on WordPress, Wix, Shopify, Blogger or a hand-made site. It sits in its own box, so it never breaks your design.

Shows what’s missing

The builder lists questions it couldn’t answer and which answers people found helpful, so you know what to add next.

Ready in three steps

  1. 1

    Add your questions

    Start from a sample shop, class or guesthouse, or from a blank list. Write each answer in English and Sinhala.

  2. 2

    Test it as you go

    The chat on the right uses your questions as you type them. Ask it the way a customer would.

  3. 3

    Copy one line to your site

    Paste the code before </body>. The chat button appears in the corner of every page.

Private by design

Everything runs in the visitor’s browser. Questions are not sent to any server or AI company, there is no cost per message, and it keeps working when traffic spikes.
